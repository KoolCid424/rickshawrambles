More than a bicycle

Cycling history, you can ride.

Forget the ordinary afternoon bike ride. Jacob's Vintage Bicycles brings together an eccentric collection of machines from different eras of cycling history.

From enormous penny farthings and classic tandems to unmistakable British icons, every bicycle has a story to tell. And, where suitable, you don't just look at it. You ride it.

The Collection

A rather unusual collection of bicycles.

Nine bicycles. Several decades of cycling history. And absolutely no shortage of things to talk about.

01

Penny Farthing

The unmistakable Victorian high-wheel bicycle. A spectacular piece of cycling history and one of the ultimate centrepieces of the collection.

02

Bone Shaker

One of cycling's earliest designs, with a fascinatingly primitive construction that shows just how far bicycles have evolved.

03

Vintage Tandem

A wonderfully social classic bicycle built for two. Perfect for sharing the experience with someone else.

04

Safety Bicycle

The design that helped shape the modern bicycle, with two similarly sized wheels and a much more familiar riding position.

05

Indonesian Rickshaw

A wonderfully unusual piece of transport history, bringing a completely different cycling culture into the collection.

06

1960s Racing Bike

Lightweight, elegant and unmistakably from the golden age of road racing.

07

Raleigh Chopper

One of the most recognisable British bicycles ever made. Instantly nostalgic and unmistakably 1970s.

08

Raleigh Grifter

A chunky British classic from the BMX-era boom, packed with character and nostalgia.

09

Pashley Mailstar

A genuine British workhorse originally designed for postal deliveries and a brilliant example of practical cycling heritage.
